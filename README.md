# Brositz_P3

# Design Journal

I have spent the most time designing the maze for level two and adding the rocks, keys and saw fish. It was most difficult learning how to implement the pathways and frames for the saw fish to function properly, so it was a lot of trial and error figuring out what part of my code was causing the glitch. 
After some testing, I found at that I needed to add * delta in order to compensate different computers for the different frames per second. 
In addition, designing the maze was an interesting process trying to decide where to put the keys and how to layout the maze to make it challenging but not overly frustrating for the player. 
For player instructions, I include a label to briefly show the goal of the levels, but in addition, I did not want to explain too much because some aspects are self explanatory such as spikes usually will show a sign of harming players. 
I also tried to use flowers as a visual affordance to point players in the right direction in level 1 and to show when they reached the portal. 
Inspiration for this game came from me playing the dungeon levels in Stardew Valley, however, I wanted this to be a more fantasy "in the forest" feel of a game rather than always trying to battle and avoid danger. 
The thought behind the design is a little slice of bread exploring the forest and going through villages to make it home.
